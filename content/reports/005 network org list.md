### International Association for Chronic Fatigue Syndrome/Myalgic Encephalomyelitis (IACFS/ME)
International non-profit of scientific, medical, and other professionals; active since 1990. 501(c)(3).
https://www.iacfsme.org/about-iacfs-me/
- **Mission:** Promote and coordinate research, patient care, and treatment exchange for ME/CFS and fibromyalgia
- **Journal:** *Fatigue: Biomedicine, Health, and Behavior* (quarterly, peer-reviewed; founded/edited by Fred Friedberg)
- **Resources:** Clinician Primer, position statements, conferences (biennial), newsletters (2026– editor Dr. Lea Höfel)
- **Leadership:** Fred Friedberg PhD (President, Stony Brook), Lily Chu MD (VP), Charles Lapp MD (Treasurer); board members in US, UK, Canada, Iceland
- Geographic focus: International

Newsletters: https://www.iacfsme.org/newsletters/

### Long Covid Advocacy (LCA)
UK non-profit social enterprise (Long Covid Advocacy Ltd, Co. no. 15794841), 100% volunteer-led.
https://www.longcovidadvoc.com/
- **Four pillars:** Legislate · Investigate · Ventilate · Educate — removing barriers for people with Long COVID and ME
- Donates all profits to biomedical LC/ME research (Polybio, ME Research UK)
- Runs toolkit, blog/101 series (Reinfection 101, Psychologisation 101), #Cripademia quarterly book club, Friends of LCA volunteer network
- Geographic focus: UK-based; international reach

## RTI ME/CFS Collaborative Research Network (ME/CFSnet)

https://mecfs.rti.org/

NIH-funded research network coordinated by RTI International, focused on ME/CFS cause, diagnosis, and treatment — none of which currently have established biomarkers or FDA-approved therapies.
Funding: NIH NIAID + NINDS (grant U24-NS-105535)

**Member institutions:** Columbia University, Cornell University, ICanCME Research Network (Canada) · Data coordinating center: RTI International

**Initiatives:**
- ME/CFS Exchange Webinar Series (quarterly, with NINDS) — first session Oct 8, 2026: post-acute infection symptoms; recording posted
- "From Mechanisms to Medicine" NIH workshop, Sept 23–24, 2026, North Bethesda, MD (researchers, clinicians, people with lived experience)

**Geographic focus:** US + Canada; worldwide framing
