How the total COMPASS 31 score is calculated

Source: codebook phd009185.3.xml. Each subscore adds up its recoded items and multiplies by a fixed weight. The total (compass31_score) is the sum of the six subscores.

SubscoreItemsFormula
Orthostatic intolerance (compass31_orthoint)01–04sum of recoded items × 4
Vasomotor (compass31_vasomot)05–07sum of recoded items × 0.8333
Secretomotor (compass31_secretmot)08–11sum of recoded items × 2.1428571
Gastrointestinal (compass31_gastro)12–23sum of recoded items × 0.8928571
Bladder (compass31_bladder)24–26sum of recoded items × 1.111
Pupillomotor (compass31_pupmot)27–31sum of recoded items × 0.333
Total (compass31_score)allsum of the six subscores

The orthostatic subscore ranges from 0 to 40. The overall range of the total score was not checked against the recode values for every subscore.

Q3 is item 03 of the COMPASS-31 questionnaire: "How severe are these feelings?" These are the lightheadedness, faintness, or dizziness on standing that item 01 asks about. The answers are Mild (scored 1), Moderate (2), or Severe (3). Participants answer it only after a Yes to item 01.

Ages 6-11 and 12-17 use the under-18 item (compass31_03). Ages 18-25 use the same item without the _ya suffix. Each bar is a share of the age group's total at that visit. Grey segments are counts under 10 and are hidden.
