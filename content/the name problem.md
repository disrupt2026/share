# Mothers Opposing Barriers

**MOB** is a parent-led advocacy organization fighting for children with chronic illness.

We oppose every barrier between a sick child and the treatment they need — medical, insurance, political, and systemic. We are not asking nicely.

---

## What we do

- **Political advocacy** — pushing for treatment access, research funding, and policy change
- **Community** — connecting parents of chronically ill children across the world
- **Action** — showing up, speaking out, and refusing to wait
